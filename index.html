Welcome, traveler

You have found the pass.

Few do. This page marks the threshold — a small hold carved into the mountainside, built and kept by hand rather than by legion. Below, you'll find the chronicle of the city as it's told around the hearth: three stones, three stories, and the hook that brings strangers to the gate in the first place. Use it as you like — a homepage, a campaign primer, or just a place to hang a lantern.

The Chronicle

Three Stones of the City

The Encircling Peaks

No road leads here. The valley sits inside a ring of unclimbable stone, and the one true path shifts with the seasons, known only to the wardens who walk it blindfolded, by memory and by count of steps.

The Founding Oath

Generations back, a company of artisans, scribes, and quiet folk swore to build somewhere the wars outside could never follow. They gave up their old names for new ones, and swore never to speak the way down to strangers.

The Silver Gate

The smiths' last and greatest work: a gate that will not open for force, coin, or clever tongue — only for a true name, spoken by someone who means to stay.

"They say lantern-light shows between the peaks on the clearest nights — and that somewhere below, a bell tolls only for the lost."
